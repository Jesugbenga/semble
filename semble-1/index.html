    Cultural Tips for Job Seekers

    United States

    • Arrive 10-15 minutes early for interviews
    • Maintain eye contact during conversations
    • Use formal language until invited to be informal

    Germany

    • Use formal titles (Herr/Frau) unless told otherwise
    • Direct communication is valued
    • Bring printed copies of your resume/CV
